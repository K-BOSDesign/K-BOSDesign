K-BOS Design

K-BOS Design opened up in February 2024 with a raging start. In the first three months, we handled logo designs, paper designs and created digital assets for various clients.

In May 2024, K-BOS Design created their first full logo package that changed the way they were viewed. MH Haulage's design was nominated for a design award by DesignRush and became a featured design on their website.

So why choose K-BOS Design?

Mission

At K-BOS Design we empower businesses to effectively reach their target audiences through sustainable, cost-effective logos and brand identities.

Vision

Our Vision is to create visual assets that are original, sustainable and attractive to the audiences of our clients. The assets will represent the values of the clients and tell the story of their business. All the visuals together build strong brand identities that will make our clients successful in their business building ventures.

Values

K-BOS Design is the business that it is due to its STIC-values. These values inform the decisions regarding the course of the business as well as inform the design choices we make with every client's design.

Sustainability

At K-BOS Design, we value the environment as much as we value ensuring your investment stands the test of time. Therefore, we design visual assets that are versatile and adaptable across various media platforms, further supporting the development and essence of your business.

Teamwork

Collaboration is at the heart of our creative process. We do not just work for you; we work with you. Together, we uncover the story of your business through teamwork and partnership. Our projects benefit from collective effort and shared vision.

Innovation

We thrive on creativity and forward-thinking solutions. Our passion for innovation drives us to push boundaries, explore new possibilities and upskill continuously. This allows us to deliver cutting-edge designs that set our clients apart.

Communication

Clear and open communication is essential to our success. We listen closely to our clients' needs and ideas, ensuring that our designs not only meet, but exceed their expectations, fostering strong and lasting relationships.
