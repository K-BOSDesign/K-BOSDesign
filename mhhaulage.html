MH Haulage

In 2024, we partnered with MH Haulage PTY LTD to enhance their brand identity.

With years of experience delivering reliable, no-nonsense services, MH Haulage promotes professionalism, simplicity and a calm approach. Our design solution was crafted to reflect these core values.

The logo comprises of a monogram combining the business's initials in one single symbol. Keeping the logo simple, recognisable and approachable. The colours have been specifically chosen to represent calmness and give the logo a trustworthy feel.

DesignRush nominated MH Haulage's logo for the Best Logo Design award in September 2025. They stated, the brand was clean and highly functional. Read more about their opinion here.
